About me

03 / SELECTED RESEARCH

Research projects

CARDIAC MRI · 3D RECONSTRUCTION

ReLAX — Personalized 3D Cardiac Reconstruction from Multi-slice CMR

A two-stage approach for reconstructing high-resolution three-dimensional cardiac anatomy from low-resolution short-axis (SAX) cardiac MRI. The first stage combines image and anatomical features for motion correction and uses super-resolution to improve through-plane resolution. The second stage adapts the reconstruction to an individual patient using long-axis (2CH/3CH/4CH) views.

Key methods: motion correction · anatomical feature fusion · 3D super-resolution · long-axis-guided personalized optimization

↗

OSTEOSARCOMA · PROGNOSTIC MODELING

Multi-scale Radiomics and Deep Learning for Osteosarcoma Prognosis

Research integrating clinical variables, multi-scale radiomics, and deep-learning features extracted from pretreatment MRI to predict two-year progression-free survival outcomes after surgery in osteosarcoma. The project explores how different information sources contribute to multimodal prognostic modeling.

Key methods: MRI radiomics · 3D deep learning · multimodal feature fusion · risk prediction

↗

EXPERIMENTAL VALIDATION

Evaluation of Cardiac Reconstruction Across Imaging Domains

Evaluating cardiac reconstruction with volumetric overlap and surface-distance metrics such as Dice and HD95, including investigation of model generalization under domain shift between cardiac MRI datasets.

Focus: quantitative validation · generalization · anatomical consistency
